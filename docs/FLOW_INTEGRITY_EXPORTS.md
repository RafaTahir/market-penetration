## Time and source semantics

- `generatedAt` records artifact creation only. It is never assigned as a market observation date.
- Economic `metricEvidence` exposes source, annual observation period, retrieval time, status and limitations supplied by the aggregation layer. Legacy record `lastUpdated` is not reinterpreted as observation evidence.
- Stock observations remain unverified when only a stored record exists. Storage time is not an observation time. FX observation dates come only from explicitly supplied `observedAt`; missing ones are Unavailable.
- Provider/source descriptions are carried as supplied provenance and do not imply independent verification, freshness, current legal applicability or suitability for a particular business. Static examples identify their actual code-file origin instead of attributing them to an external authority.
